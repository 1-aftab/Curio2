The product

Built to be difficult to dismiss.

Not a sleep score. A clock that refuses to be polite about the morning.

01

The side button fails.

Power turns the screen off for a moment, at most. Home and a swiped notification do the same. The tone keeps going.

02

The challenge is the exit.

Math, a memory sequence, or a phrase. Easy, medium, or hard. Turn on more than one and Curio picks at random.

03

Snooze is not a lifestyle.

5, 9, or 15 minutes, up to three times. Kiosk mode never snoozes. After the last one, only the challenge stops it.

Quick alarms

15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour from home. One-shot, with a single 9-minute snooze.

Wake streak

Consecutive days you actually solved it. Snooze does not count. A timer does not count.

Your alert

Sound and vibration, sound only, or vibration only. Import your own audio. Volume can fade in.

Timer

Rings with the app closed or the phone locked. One tap stops it. Alarms do not get that mercy.

Stopwatch

Laps, with the fastest and slowest marked. It keeps the right time if you leave.

Private by default

No account, no ads, no tracking. Permissions are explained before they are asked, and they exist so it can ring.

Install

On your phone in a minute.

Direct install. Not the Play Store. Android 8.0 or newer.

01

Download

Save Curio Alarm. Version 1.3. Send it to the phone if you downloaded it on a computer.

02

Open the file

If Android blocks it, allow installs from your browser or Files. Play Protect may warn you. That is because it is not the store.

03

Open Curio

Read the consent screen first. The checkbox is required. Then allow exact alarms if you want the minute you set.
